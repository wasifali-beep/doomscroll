## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

First release.

### Added
- Swipe feed of concept cards in Roman Urdu with English tech terms.
- Topics: backend/networking, databases, mixed tech, and custom topics.
- Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.
- Daily goal (5, 10, 20 cards). Feed stops at the goal; "5 aur" extends it.
- Card actions: simplify, expand, more examples, use cases, free-form question.
- Quiz after every 5 cards, "Yaad hai?" review quizzes, and a 5-question test.
- Spaced repetition (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days) with a "dohrane hain" queue on Home.
- Saved cards.
- Stats: streak, quiz accuracy, mastered cards, 7-day chart, topic counts.
- Daily reminder notification.
- Read-aloud voice with speed setting.
- Offline pack of 20 cards.
- Claude and Gemini support, key stored on device only.
- Version shown at the bottom of Settings.
